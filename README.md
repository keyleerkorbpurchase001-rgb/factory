# Plant Machinery Portal

Three complete production plants — an electronics & display plant, a refrigerator manufacturing plant and an automotive press hardening plant — with every machine of each plant placed in its production line.

* **Live site:** https://marketing-in.github.io/plant-machinery-portal/
* `index.html` – the portal (plant overviews, plant layouts, equipment categories, machine lists, brochures)
* `<hall>.html` – 18 live 3D halls (three.js); `hall.html#L2` opens line 2 selected
* `machine-rules.js` – the logic that decides what every placed machine looks like: for each catalogue row (station, machine name, weight, count) an ordered rule table (station role → make / model → factory-line text rules → weight class) picks a model kind and its source: a factory-line line model (`plant`), a factory-line builder (`fl`), one of the portal's own builders (`mb`, in `machines/`) or the page's own housing (`none`). `MR.audit(HALL_DATA)` reports the coverage. `machines/common.js` holds the shared helpers (screens with the catalogue card, keypads, e-stops, lamp towers, doors, hinges, grilles, nameplates, brand bands, cable chains, pallets, magazines…), `machines/KINDS.md` the catalogue of the portal's own kinds (real sizes, features, the catalogue photos each copies), `machines/handling.js` (SMT board handling and conveyors), `machines/logistics.js` (packing and warehouse), `machines/metal.js` (shot blasting, laser cells, saws, mould change, benches), `machines/lab.js` (instrument racks, measuring and inspection stations) and `machines/dress.js` (the detail pass that adds doors, controls, labels, hoses and fences to the plain factory-line models). Test a single model on the bench: `tools/model_test.html?kind=laserCell&src=mb&w=9&d=5&h=3.4&name=Trumpf+TruLaser+Cell+8030` (served on 127.0.0.1:8080), or `node tools/model_shot.mjs "kind=…&src=mb&…" out/name iso,front,close` for screenshots.
* `hall-kit.js`, `hall.css` – the design of the hall pages (from the factory-line project): detailed walls (steel columns with striped guards, windows, louvres, cable tray, sprinkler and air lines, floodlights, electrical boards, fire points, first aid, signs, roll-up doors with bollards, fire exit, personnel door, clock), the zone painted round the machines with the hall's name, and the page chrome (toolbar, legend, stats, station chips, machine details, the other halls). `hall-data.js` is generated from `index.html` by `tools/make_hall_data.py`. `fl-models.js` is the factory-line machine library (SMT machines, presses, robots, dryers, ovens, CNC, testers, lab, heavy and general machines) bundled by `tools/bundle_fl.mjs` from `../factory-line/src`; the kit stands these models in place of a hall's own housings wherever the library has the machine's kind, and gives every other placed machine its catalogue photograph as its front, its real colours, a maker nameplate, grilles and feeders or stacks where they belong
* `tools/render_views.mjs` – re-renders every hall's plan view (`top/`, shown on the plant layout tab; `?top=1`) and preview photo (`?shot=1`) with headless Chrome, `tools/apply_sizes.py` writes the plan sizes into `index.html`; `tools/patch_halls.py` and `tools/patch_index_2d.py` are the one-off patches that wired the kit into the pages and the hall envelope into the 2D station map
* `photos/` – original machine photographs and catalogue sheets
* `*.xlsx` – machine lists, `*.pdf` – brochures

Machine data and photographs come from the plants' public equipment listings. Line groupings, station assignments and categories are an engineering assessment; availability, condition and terms are to be confirmed with each seller.
